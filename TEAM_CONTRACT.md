# Team Contract — Church Review App

## Team Members: 
**Ama, Beyla, Vitus, Ryan, Bodie**

## Expectations
- Communicate constantly — flag roadblocks or conflicts as soon as they come up, don't sit on them.
- Weekly time commitment: as many hours as it takes to finish your assigned work, typically 3–8 hrs/week, with work evenly split across the team.
- Punctuality: be at meetings on time, ideally 5 minutes early, so meetings start immediately.
- Unexpected commitments (illness, interviews, etc.): communicate ASAP via Teams; team redistributes work as needed, with more flexibility built in as the semester's project load increases.

## Communication
- Primary channel: Microsoft Teams.
- Expected response time: within 24 hours.
- Be transparent — no silent slipping on deadlines.

## Meeting Schedule
- Tuesday, 6:15–6:45pm, Hekman Library. Anyone who can't attend in person joins via Teams.

## Responsibilities
- Each 3-week sprint has a Project Manager (runs meetings, admin tasks) and a Lead Engineer (PR reviews/merges).
- Roles rotate every sprint.
- Task assignment: objectives are assigned per sprint. If you're blocked and won't finish in time, ask for help immediately. If you have spare capacity, offer it.

## Equitable Contribution & Conflict Resolution
- Conflicts are mediated by the PM or LE — stay respectful and open-minded, disagree on ideas, not people.
- If a teammate misses two deadlines without communicating in advance, they must explain to the team why. A third missed deadline without communication means the team brings in the professor.
