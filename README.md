## Vision Statement

The Church Match app is for College Students who struggle to find church communities away from home. The Church Match App is a suggestion generating and experience quantifying app that provides suggestions based on user parameters and feedback from previous church suggestions. Unlike utilizing google reviews for churches,or word of mouth from upperclassmen our app will provide greater catering to an individuals theological or experiential preferences and additionally will provide services for the user once they have committed to a church community for becoming more active.
